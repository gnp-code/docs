Magpie Directive is a centralized knowledge hub that acts as your company's single source of truth. Whether your team is onboarding new hires, answering support questions, or feeding structured context into AI pipelines, Magpie Directive gives every human and agent a consistent, reliable place to find exactly what they need.

## The Problems Magpie Directive Solves

Knowledge inside most organizations is scattered across wikis, Slack threads, Google Docs, and people's heads. This creates three compounding problems: **scattered knowledge** that's hard to find and easy to lose, **inconsistent answers** as different team members reference different versions of the truth, and **expensive AI context retrieval** when LLMs need to pull relevant information from unstructured, unindexed sources.

Magpie Directive solves all three. It gives you a structured, searchable, API-first knowledge base that serves both your people and your AI systems from a single, authoritative store.
