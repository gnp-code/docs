## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up for Magpie Directive and log in to your dashboard. Your workspace is ready immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first knowledge entry">
    Use the dashboard or the REST API to add content to your knowledge base. Structure it with tags and categories for easy retrieval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and retrieve">
    Query your knowledge base using full-text or semantic search — directly from the UI or via API calls from your applications and AI agents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Set up webhooks and integrations to keep Magpie Directive in sync with your existing workflows and tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>
