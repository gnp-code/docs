## What it is

Receiving is the accounts-payable side of Magpie Directive. It covers **what vendors bill us**. Vendor invoices and credit memos come in, get checked against what was ordered and what actually arrived, and are locked once a person verifies them. Payment is a separate build.

<Note>
Goods receipt, meaning receiving product into inventory, stays in **Purchase Orders** because it needs its direct tie to orders. Receiving works with the vendor's paperwork against those receipts.
</Note>

## How an invoice arrives

Every invoice keeps its **source file** attached beside the digitized record. The record is what gets matched; the file is the evidence.

| Source | How it's digitized | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| Hand entry | Pre-filled from the order | [MAG-198](https://linear.app/magpiedirective/issue/MAG-198) |
| Inbound EDI 810 | From the EDI service | [MAG-201](https://linear.app/magpiedirective/issue/MAG-201) |
| Emailed PDF or image | Extracted automatically, then verified by a person | [MAG-202](https://linear.app/magpiedirective/issue/MAG-202) |

## Three-way match

Each invoice line is checked against the **ordered** price and quantity, what was **received** after corrections, and any **accepted credits** ([MAG-170](https://linear.app/magpiedirective/issue/MAG-170)).

**Tolerances cascade.** The organization sets a default that applies to every vendor, and each vendor can be adjusted independently. Per-account tolerances aren't supported yet.

## Credits

Credits arrive in two ways, and both are supported ([MAG-199](https://linear.app/magpiedirective/issue/MAG-199)):

- **As an adjustment** on the invoice itself.
- **As a separate credit memo** later. Universities often pay the original invoice in full and receive the credit afterwards.

Either way, the credit matches against the original invoice, and against the order where possible. A credit memo is its own document, never a negative line on the next invoice.

## Separation of duties

Whoever places orders cannot reconcile invoices ([MAG-180](https://linear.app/magpiedirective/issue/MAG-180)).

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Default | **By role.** Ordering roles cannot reconcile invoices or credit memos. |
| Per-person option | An organization can also turn on a check so that no one can reconcile an invoice for an order they placed. |
| Override | **Organization admins** can apply it. It is audited and shown on the record. |
| Owners | Can perform every action. |

## Verify and lock

A **person** verifies a matched invoice ([MAG-200](https://linear.app/magpiedirective/issue/MAG-200)). Agents can enter invoices and run matches, but they cannot verify.

Once verified, the **whole record locks**: no adjustments and no credits posted against it. **Only an admin can unlock** it, and every unlock is audited.
